# Physics Research Execution

Execute the most informative physical route, then follow the feedback. Make
equations, units, assumptions, and boundary/initial conditions explicit.
Report residuals, convergence, uncertainty, provenance, and evidence limits
when they matter to the claim. A toy, a metadata record, or a workflow
byproduct is not physical evidence.
