# Physics Research Planning

Choose the route from the physics and expected information gain. Keep multiple
mechanisms alive when uncertainty warrants it, and turn weak feedback into a
changed route rather than more paperwork. Delegate cohesive claim-bearing work
and reuse settled evidence.
