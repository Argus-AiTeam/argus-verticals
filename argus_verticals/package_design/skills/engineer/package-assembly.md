# Physical evidence has its own scope

Identify the proposed process sequence and the applicable material, dimensional
and handling requirements. Die attach, wire/bump connection, encapsulation,
substrate attachment and final assembly may each introduce tolerances and
defect mechanisms. Do not invent a vendor-qualified process window.

Plan traceable inspection and test against specific risks: alignment, bond
quality, voids, interconnect continuity, known-good-die status and accessible
test interfaces. A simulated ideal contact does not establish actual contact
quality, and a passing connectivity test does not prove long-term reliability.

Qualification needs a stated specification, samples, conditions and acceptance
criteria. Distinguish simulation, prototype characterization, accelerated
testing and production screening. Extrapolation to lifetime requires a
defensible failure mechanism and model.

The native reference models are original synthetic examples, not specimens.
No manufacturing order, commercial-tool license use, physical operation or
qualification campaign is implied by completing a thermal study.
