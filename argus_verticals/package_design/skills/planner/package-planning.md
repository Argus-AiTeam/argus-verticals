# Plan the requested question, not an entire product

Separate package architecture, model preparation, selected thermal studies and
independent review. An existing-model thermal request does not require a new
bump map, substrate layout, mechanical analysis or manufacturing campaign.
Select the smallest matching profile or composed scope.

Check material provenance, units, ideal interfaces, heat-source distribution
and cooling conditions before asking for numerical work. Preserve source-edit
permissions and all original engineering bounds. Do not replace unsupported
physics with a convenient ideal model without explicitly obtaining agreement.

Require a genuine coarse/fine comparison for lateral spreading. Keep model,
power and base temperature fixed while changing mesh size; otherwise the
comparison measures more than discretization. Declare a metric and absolute
tolerance in its own unit.

Broader package knowledge should identify missing electrical, mechanical,
process and physical evidence. It must not turn those gaps into fictitious
completed stages.
