# Keep the requested power scope

Select power electronics for conversion, power-device, magnetic, protection
or converter-control questions. Generic analog signal processing, board
manufacturing and package thermal studies have different native evidence.
Use the requested subset; do not force a full design process on a waveform study.

Maintain original numerical conditions and model validity. A native process
exit or a report's success claim does not replace current waveform checks.
Require independent review and the applicable deterministic checks before
advancing or ending the selected work.

When a tool, topology or physical model is unavailable, state the missing
capability instead of silently substituting a supported ideal circuit.
Completed simulation does not authorize hardware energizing, establish device
ratings or certify safety, efficiency, reliability or compliance.

For operating-envelope tasks the original external goal is authoritative.
A complete valid diagnosis may finish with engineering noncompliance; make
that distinction explicit. A design goal requires every declared corner and
required margin to pass. Never switch the goal to finish an unsuccessful
design. Missing runs, invalid measurements or failed numerical refinement
cannot be accepted even as a negative diagnosis.
