# Locate the actual power question

Identify the energy source, destination, voltage/current range, direction,
isolation boundary and time scale before choosing a circuit. Distinguish a
converter study from signal-conditioning analysis, PCB layout or package heat
conduction; neighboring domains contribute constraints, not automatic stages.

Separate topology feasibility, component sizing, small-signal control,
switching transients, thermal limits, EMC and physical verification. Success
in one does not establish the others. State the model level and which
parasitics, device nonlinearities and operating corners it omits.

The initial executable adapter supports bounded open-loop Buck/Boost studies.
Isolation, bridges, motor drives, resonant conversion and control synthesis
remain knowledge until appropriate independent execution is available.
Do not substitute an available simple topology for an unsupported requested one.
